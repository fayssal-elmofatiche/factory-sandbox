title: Support block orders across several hours
state: open
labels:
author: demo
created: 2026-01-01T00:00:04Z
---

Power plants that are expensive to start want to sell a fixed volume over
several consecutive hours, or not at all. Add block offers: one volume and one
limit price for a range of delivery hours, for example 200 MWh in each hour
from 08:00 to 20:00 at 70 €/MWh. A block is accepted in every hour of its
range or in none of them, and the clearing should take blocks into account.
