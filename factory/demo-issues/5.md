After the auction the market operator settles every trade and needs a file of
the results. Add a download of the day's orders as CSV, one row per order,
with the order id, participant, side, delivery hour, limit price, volume
offered, volume accepted and the hour's clearing price.

Orders priced better than the clearing price are accepted in full, orders
priced worse are rejected, and orders at exactly the clearing price share the
remaining volume pro rata. The accepted volumes of each hour must add up to
the cleared volume on both the bid and the offer side.
