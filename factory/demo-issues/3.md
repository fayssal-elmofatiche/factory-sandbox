Today orders only come from the seed data. Add a form to the prices page where
a trader enters participant, side (bid or offer), delivery hour, volume in MWh
and limit price in €/MWh, and submits it to the server. A valid order goes into
the order book and the trader sees the updated prices with a confirmation
message. An invalid order is not stored, and the form shows what is wrong next
to the field it concerns, keeping what the trader typed.

Traders also need to withdraw an order they placed before the auction closes.
