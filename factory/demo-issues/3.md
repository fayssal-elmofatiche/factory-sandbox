When a price looks odd, analysts want to see why. Make each hour on the prices
page a link to a page for that hour, for example `/hours/18`, that shows:

- the supply curve (offers stacked cheapest first) and the demand curve (bids
  stacked dearest first) as a step chart, volume in MWh across and price in
  €/MWh up, with the clearing point marked;
- which order is marginal, that is the one that sets the price, by its side,
  price and volume (not by participant, which is confidential);
- the clearing price, the cleared volume, and the number of bids and offers.

An hour with no orders, or one that did not clear, says so plainly. An hour
outside 0 to 23, or a path that is not a number, returns 404. The prices page
and the hour page link to each other.
