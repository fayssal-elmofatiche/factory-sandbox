Traders read a day-ahead result as a shape, not as 24 rows. Above the table on
the prices page, draw the day's clearing prices as a chart: one bar per
delivery hour, coloured so the peak hours (08:00 to 20:00) stand apart from
the off-peak hours, with a horizontal line each for the base price (average of
all hours that cleared) and the peak price (average of the peak hours that
cleared), both labelled with their value in €/MWh.

Hours that did not clear have no bar and are marked as such. The chart must
work without JavaScript or external libraries (inline SVG is fine), be
readable on a phone, and give screen-reader users the same information, for
example a text summary of base, peak, the highest and the lowest hour.
