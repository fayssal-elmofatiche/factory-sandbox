Traders quote a day-ahead result as two numbers before anything else: base and
peak. The order book clears each hour but cannot give either.

Add both to the order book. Base is the plain average of the clearing prices of
all delivery hours that cleared. Peak is the same average over the peak hours
only, 08:00 to 20:00 (delivery hours 8 to 19). Each is `None` when no hour in
its range cleared. Hours without a clearing price do not count as zero.

Do not show them on the prices page yet; the request for the chart does that.
